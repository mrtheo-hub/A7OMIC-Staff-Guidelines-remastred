Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 5, 2026

This explains what the A7OMIC Staff Guidelines portal (the "Portal") collects when you sign in with Discord, and what it deliberately doesn't.

1. What we collect

When you sign in, Discord's OAuth process shares a limited slice of your account with us, scoped to exactly what's needed to check that you're staff:

DataWhy
Discord user IDIdentifies your session
Your roles in the A7OMIC serverConfirms you hold the staff role

That's it from Discord. We request the identify, guilds, and guilds.members.read OAuth scopes and nothing broader.

Separately, the login page runs a Cloudflare Turnstile check to confirm you're not a bot. Turnstile processes some technical signals from your browser (things like how your device behaves, not personal content) to make that call; Cloudflare's own Privacy Policy governs that processing, not this one.

If something on the login flow looks like tampering, a forged link, or an automated attack rather than a normal login, your IP address and browser User-Agent string are captured for that one event and sent to a private security channel we monitor. This does not happen on a normal successful login, only when something is flagged as suspicious.

2. What we don't collect

  • No email address
  • No message content, DMs, or server activity
  • No analytics or behavioral tracking on normal usage
  • No advertising cookies or ad-network trackers
  • No payment or financial information

3. How it's used

Primarily to answer one question: does this Discord account currently hold the staff role in the A7OMIC server? If yes, you get in. If no, you're sent back to the login page. The security-event data described above (IP/User-Agent, only on suspicious activity) is used solely to help us notice and respond to attacks on the login system, never for tracking normal staff behavior.

4. How it's stored

Your Discord ID is placed inside a single signed, httpOnly cookie stored in your own browser. That's the only place your identity lives day-to-day. There's no database on our end and no server-side record of who's logged in. Security-event alerts (only for flagged suspicious activity) are delivered as one-off messages to a Discord channel, they aren't written to any database, but they do persist there under Discord's own message retention until someone manually deletes them.

5. Retention

The session cookie expires automatically after 24 hours, or 30 days if you checked "Remember me" at login. Logging out clears it immediately regardless of which duration was set. After expiry or logout, nothing about that session persists anywhere.

6. Sharing

We don't sell or rent this data to anyone. Three outside services see a limited slice of activity as a normal part of how the Portal works: Discord (identity and role check), Cloudflare (the Turnstile bot check), and, only when something suspicious is flagged, a Discord webhook we control that posts the alert. Each provider's own privacy policy governs their handling of that data on their end.

The cookie notice on the login page groups these into two categories: Essential (session and login security, can't be disabled) and Remember me (optional, controls whether the 30-day option is available at all). None of these are advertising or cross-site tracking cookies.

8. Your choices

You can end a session anytime with the Log out link. You can turn off the "Remember me" cookie category from the Cookie Settings option in the login notice. You can also revoke this app's access entirely from your own Discord account under User Settings → Authorized Apps, effective immediately regardless of any active session here.

9. Changes to this policy

If what we collect or how we use it changes, this page will be updated and the date at the top will move.
