Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 23, 2026

This explains what the A7OMIC Staff Guidelines portal (the "Portal") collects when you sign in with Discord, and what it deliberately doesn't.

1. What we collect

When you sign in, Discord's OAuth process shares a limited slice of your account with us, scoped to exactly what's needed to check that you're staff:

DataWhy
Discord user IDIdentifies your session
Your roles in the A7OMIC serverConfirms you hold the staff role

That's it. We request the identify and guilds.members.read OAuth scopes and nothing broader.

2. What we don't collect

  • No email address
  • No message content, DMs, or server activity
  • No analytics or tracking scripts
  • No advertising cookies or third-party trackers
  • No payment or financial information

3. How it's used

Solely to answer one question: does this Discord account currently hold the staff role in the A7OMIC server? If yes, you get in. If no, you're sent back to the login page. Nothing collected here is used for any other purpose.

4. How it's stored

Your Discord ID is placed inside a single signed, httpOnly cookie stored in your own browser. That's the only place it lives. There's no database on our end, no server-side record of who's logged in, and no log of your login history beyond what your hosting provider retains for basic operational purposes.

5. Retention

The session cookie expires automatically after 24 hours. Logging out clears it immediately. After expiry or logout, nothing about that session persists anywhere.

6. Sharing

We don't sell, rent, or share this data with any third party. Discord's own handling of your account is governed separately by Discord's Privacy Policy, which applies regardless of whether you use this Portal.

7. Cookies

One cookie: the session token described above. Strictly functional, not used for tracking or advertising.

8. Your choices

You can end a session anytime with the Log out link. You can also revoke this app's access entirely from your own Discord account under User Settings → Authorized Apps, which is effective immediately regardless of any active session here.

9. Changes to this policy

If what we collect or how we use it changes, this page will be updated and the date at the top will move.

10. Contact

Questions about this policy go to your RDOM or Executive Director.

Written to accurately describe what this specific Portal does today, not as a substitute for independent legal advice. Worth a real legal review if the Portal ever grows beyond internal staff use.
